## Why these two should look different

The **Directory** is about **discovery and desire**. It should feel like a secret market where the visitor browses opportunities and smells leverage in the air.

The **Analyzer** is about **discipline and danger detection**. It should feel like the machine that slaps the buyer before they make an emotional purchase.

Together:

```text id="vm6uuk"
Directory = “Find the deal.”
Analyzer = “Don’t get murdered by the deal.”
```

That’s the design language. Not vanilla fintech. More like **cash-flow noir with a calculator knife.**
